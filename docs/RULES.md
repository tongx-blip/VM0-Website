# The rules, in one place

`design-principles.md` argues them, `design-system.md` gives the values,
`motion.md` gives the timings, `qa-checklist.md` machine-checks them. **This
file is the index** — every rule this page is held to, one line each, with a
pointer to where it is argued and where it is checked.

If you are about to change something visual, read this. If you are about to
add a rule, add it here too, or it will be re-litigated in three rounds' time.

---

## Shape

| # | Rule | Why | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Every component with a box is a rectangle** — `--r-btn` 10px. `--r-pill` is only for things that are actually round: avatars, status dots, bar caps. | A page that mixes lozenges and rectangles has two ideas about what a control is. | QA §4j |
| S2 | **Every section is a white card on the grey page** — `--r-section` 16px, no shadow. The hero and the closing CTA band are the two deliberate exceptions. | Mixing grey bands, white bands and cards makes a reader work out what a section is three different ways. | QA §4j |
| S19 | **Sections may share ONE card when they are one movement** — `.megacard`. They keep their ids, headings, claims and reveals; what they give up is three grounds. The seam gives back half its block padding, or the join pays it twice. | The wall, the ask and the outputs are one sentence — here is everything it reaches, here is where you ask, here is what comes back — and three cards put a grey gap inside it. S2 is still the default; this is the written exception. | QA §3 |
| S3 | **A surface is a fill.** Not an outline, and not a shadow either at section scale. Shadow is reserved for a product window sitting on a section — *not* the header, which separates by tone. | A shadow heavy enough to lift a white bar off a white section reads as a bruise; a light one does not lift it at all. | QA §2 |
| S4 | **No structural lines.** Separate with grounds, space and the type scale. Exceptions are written down and scoped to one component by name — currently `.tplwin` (a browser window's own edge), `.vsui` (the platform `Card`'s 1px gray-200 border and the RunningIndicator's ripple ring), and the 0.5px ring carried as the first layer of `--e-fig` / `--e-lift`, which is a surface's own edge rather than a divider and only reads where two of them overlap. | Hairlines used as structure read as clutter. A product mock's borders are the app's chrome, not this page's. | QA §2 |
| S5 | **A box inset to a section card's width takes a section card's corner** — `--r-section`, 16px. The old `--r-nav = --r-btn + --nav-pad` derived the header's corner from the control inside it and produced 22px, which on a 54px bar is a lozenge. | The relationship being stated does not make the result right; a corner belongs to the box's own scale, not its contents'. | QA §4o |
| S6 | **The header has two states and the change between them is the message.** At rest it is full-bleed, flush, square, part of the page. Once the page moves it steps down by `--nav-top`, pulls in to `--card-gap`, takes `--r-section` and condenses to `--nav-h-stuck`. | A rounded bar floating above the first pixel of an unscrolled page is a decoration pretending to be a response to scroll. | QA §4o |
| S7 | **The header's inset is a constant; the FLOATING bar is as wide as its own content.** `--nav-pad-x` is a number, not an expression on the measure, and `.is-stuck` insets by `50% − --nav-w-stuck/2` on both sides — measured off the three groups inside and written to the bar, never read back from it. | Derived from the measure, the distance from the wordmark to the window edge changed at every width and was ~250px at 2560; and a floating bar inset to the section cards was a second full-width band. A frame's inset is a constant, and an object is the size of what it holds. | QA §4o |
| S8 | **The blur under the header is a ramp, not a switch — and the ramp starts at the top edge.** Four masked backdrop layers of increasing radius, every mask monotonic (opaque, one fade, gone) with stops as percentages of the whole veil. | A single masked layer fades the blur's *opacity* and still starts it at full strength. Masks that rise **and** fall make each layer a band, and overlapping bands print hard horizontal lines. Holding every layer at full alpha until a hem near the bottom makes the header region a flat slab — invisible under an opaque bar, obvious the moment the bar lifts and insets. | QA §4o |
| S11 | **A bare wrapper around an `<svg>` is a LINE BOX, not a box the glyph fills.** An svg is inline, so the wrapper is the glyph plus the font's descender space; centring that box rides the glyph high. Make the wrapper `inline-flex`, never the svg `display:block`. | The language control's globe sat 2.8px above its own label and chevron, which are flex items with no line box of their own. `.pref__btn svg{display:block}` is (0,1,1) and would out-specify `.pref__moon{display:none}` (0,1,0), showing the sun and the moon at once. | QA §4ab |
| S12 | **A control's label never wraps once its bar is sized to its content.** | `scrollWidth` floors to an integer on three fractional groups, so the first measurement came out 4px short and broke "GET STARTED" onto two lines. | QA §4o |
| S13 | **A state the CSS can express is dead until something sets it.** Grep the attribute the observer looks for. | `.nav.is-dark` — a whole second copy of the header, five tokens, a rootMargin and an IntersectionObserver — could never fire: nothing in the markup carried `data-ground="dark"`. It shipped that way for weeks and no gate looks for a class that is *absent*. | QA §4o 
| S16 | **A secondary button is ONE STEP off the ground it sits on, and which step that is depends on the ground.** `--wash-2` on the white section cards, `--paper` on the closing band and in the header — whose own ground IS `--wash-2` once stuck. | Making every secondary a `--wash-2` fill put a wash-2 chip on a wash-2 bar: a button you cannot see. The outlined version it replaced had the opposite fault — it was the heaviest thing in the header. | QA §4o |
| S17 | **One hover for every control, and it is ONE STEP OF THAT CONTROL'S OWN COLOUR.** Each control declares its resting fill as `--fill` and paints from it; hover is `color-mix(--state-ink --hover-step, --fill)`, a 1px lift and `--e-btn`. Derived, never picked — and never a swap to a different colour. | First there were four events: a .66-alpha orange glow, a .35 ink one, a send button that swapped to a dark brown, a sign-in that rolled its label. Correcting that to "no fill changes colour" left five controls whose hovered computed style was *identical* to their resting one — the send button most visibly (*"怎么一点hover效果都不给了"*). An event and no event are both wrong answers; a step is the same button, acknowledged. | QA §4aj |
| S18 | **Uppercase marks a label, never a control — and a control is set in the PROSE register.** Eyebrows, tags, status chips, the footer's legal strip and data labels are uppercase mono, tracked; every control — nav link, button, tab, preference — is `--fb` 500 at `--t-meta` (`--t-sm` on `.btn--lg`), sentence case, `letter-spacing:normal`, no trailing-space compensation. | The header shouted GET STARTED / SIGN IN over a headline in sentence case and a product mock that has always been sentence case (P5): *"header和所有button不要全都大写"*. Taking only the capitals off exposed the other two thirds of that register — a fixed advance gives `i` the width of `m`, so the lowercase words spaced visibly unevenly and the face read as a typewriter next to the mock's proportional 14px controls: *"字体用的对么？为什么字间距都不一样？"*. Mono, capitals and tracking are one register; a control leaves all three at once. | QA §4aj |
| S10 | **Figure shadows come from `--e-fig` / `--e-lift`, never picked per surface.** One for a card resting on a ground, one for a card floating in front of another. | Eight surfaces had drifted to second-layer alphas between .24 and .5 — each chosen next to the last rather than against a scale, and the heaviest was twice `--e-2`. | QA §4ab |
| S9 | **A delimited strip does not need a surface of its own.** A footer with a divider above it and the card's own inset either side is already delimited; a ring-and-shadow bar inside it is a card inside a card. | The bar sat 3px off the edge of the card containing it. Measured first: the 3px was not the fault — the surface was. | QA §4z |
| S14 | **The floating header carries a 0.5px inset ring, and it is the one outline on the page.** Written as `box-shadow:inset`, never `border` — the stuck bar's width is computed in JS from its contents, and a border adds to the box on both sides. It arrives with the ground, not before it. | The bar is `#EAEEF0` on a `#F4F6F7` page: contrast **1.077**, the same size as the page's own step from grey to a white card, so the header separated from the page by exactly as much as the page separates from itself. Shadow was refused twice for the right reason — heavy enough to beat 1.077 is a bruise, and reaching for one anyway is *廉价*. | QA §4ai |
| S15 | **`--ink-rgb` and `--paper-rgb` do not flip in the dark theme.** The dark block overrides `--ink` as a hex and leaves the channels alone, so anything built on `rgb(var(--ink-rgb) / a)` keeps LIGHT ink there. Scrims and state tints want that; **edges do not**. Declare an edge explicitly per theme. | The sign-in button's ring has been a *darker* line on a dark bar since it was written — a groove instead of an outline — and the new header edge would have inherited it. | QA §4ai |

## Measure

| # | Rule | Why | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **One section geometry**: `--card-gap` (grey around the card, **on all four sides**) and `--pad-section` (card edge → content), with the block padding derived at `× 1.35`. The gap between two sections is the gap beside them. | A card whose top gap has no relationship to its side gap reads as two decisions. | QA §4j |
| M2 | **The measure is capped at 1320px** — and the cap lives *inside* `padding-inline: max(--pad-section, --edge − --card-gap)`. Setting that to `--pad-section` alone silently removes it. | | QA §4j2 |
| M3 | **The header is exactly as wide as a section card.** | A header sized by its own expression agrees with the cards at no width at all. | QA §4j |
| M5 | **`height` on a grid container is not a ceiling.** An implicit row track is `auto`, so the tallest item pushes the frame past the height you declared. Say `grid-template-rows:minmax(0,1fr)`, and `min-height:0` on the children. | Seven tabs came out 545 / 552 / 574: the section jumped as you moved along the strip. | QA §4ab |
| M6 | **A column beside a fixed-width object takes that object's width, not the leftover track.** | The Cloud browser card caps itself at 400px the way the product does; a `1fr` column left it sitting against 200px of nothing with the captions running past it on both sides. | QA §4j |
| M4 | **Equal air above and below a pinned block is derived**, not chosen — and it measures against the **stuck** header height, not the resting one. | A fixed top margin balances at one window height only. | QA §4j |
| M7 | **Two gaps that must look the same have to BE the same declaration** — and they are measured to the **ink**, not to the line box. A line box is taller than the letters and the letters are not centred in it, so equal `gap` and `padding` still read unequal. | The figures strip took its air above from the frame’s `row-gap` and its air below from `--o-fpad`: 15 against 27.36, which measured 18 over and 28 under on every tab. Read as *"这个文字上边的空间和下边的空间要一样高"*. | QA §4ag |
| M8 | **A bottom-anchored conversation ends on a floor, and the floor is that window's own inset** — the number its *composer* is inset by, not whatever the list happens to carry (18px on the Okou window, `clamp(14px,1.4vw,18px)` on the Slack one, which puts no inline padding on its list at all). It must beat the row gap. | 0 put the newest message's last pixel and the composer's first on the same pixel, so an artifact card ended by running into a control. 4px had been tried and read as the message being *unfinished* — because 4 is less than the 14px between two messages, and a gap smaller than the one inside the conversation says "this is still the conversation". | QA §4m3 |
| M9 | **A carousel's unit is derived from its band, and the token is declared where BOTH the card and the track inherit it.** The card reads it for its width, the track reads it for its pan; a `flex` override at a breakpoint moves one and not the other. And the item needs `min-width:0` — `min-width:auto` is min-content and beats the basis. | `flex:0 0 320px` carried the reasoning "two lanes and 14 of gap leave a 72px peek of the third", which needs a 974px band; this one is fluid 300–428. Declared on `.lane`, a 242px card panned by a 209px pitch drifted 45px further off centre every beat. Without `min-width:0` the basis computed at 244.8 / 210.8 / 174.08 and the card rendered 247 at all three. | QA §4ak |

## Type

| # | Rule | Why | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Two regions.** Reading (12 · 13.5 · 15 · 17 · 21, ratio ≈1.12, fixed px) and display (23 · 30 · 54 · 66 · 96 · 108, ratio ≈1.3, all fluid). | One ratio cannot serve a 12px label and a 108px headline. Prose must not resize with the window or it stops honouring its measure. | QA §5 |
| T2 | **The floor**: no page-level prose under 15px, no page-level label under 12px. Product mocks are exempt — they draw the app's sizes. | | QA §5 |
| T3 | **11 distinct sizes on the page, every one a token.** A one-off `clamp()` inside a rule is a bug. | It was 19 before this was written down. | QA §5 |
| T4 | **Every site-level title is Roobert 500.** Nothing outside a mock is 700; 600 survives only on the wordmark. | A bold grotesque at display size reads as shouting. | QA §4i |
| T5 | **One face, three registers**: Roobert TRIAL for display, prose and utility. The utility register is the same variable font at `MONO:100`, not a second family — so it keeps a fixed advance and a column of `09:12` still lines up. It carries labels, data and captions; **controls are Body** (S18), because a fixed advance only reads as a register while the string is uppercase. No exceptions, including inside the product mocks. | Six families were loaded and three of them were only fallbacks for each other. A register carried by size, weight and tracking survives a rebrand; one carried by a second licence does not. | QA §4i |
| T6 | **No emoji.** | It was the one pictogram on a page that has none, and it read as decoration bolted onto a sentence. | — |
| T7 | **A tracked label in a pill compensates its trailing space** — `padding-right: calc(pad - Nem)` where N is that component's own tracking. `letter-spacing` is applied after the last glyph too, so a centred tracked label is off-centre by one tracking-unit, always to the right. | Reported as "left small, right big" across every small tag on the page. | QA §4t |
| T8 | **A fixed-width object beside elastic text takes its own line once the measure runs out.** Text does not wrap *around* a chip — it wraps into two-word lines and stops being a sentence. | At 390 the scope chip turned "Create and update campaign budgets." into four two-word lines wrapped around it. | QA §4j |

## Colour

| # | Rule | Why | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **One accent, and under the brand palette it is a FILL ONLY.** `--accent` (#F8A101, the brand orange) is 2.08:1 on white — it fails as text at *every* size, including display, and the thing on it is `--accent-ink` (7.68:1), never white. All accent TEXT takes `--accent-solid` (#9A5A00: 5.47 paper, 5.06 wash, 4.59 wash-2). The old vivid #ED4E01 could carry white and clear 3:1 at display size; losing both is what makes the brand orange calmer. `--accent-wash` for accent text on a grey. | `--accent-solid` is tuned to *exactly* 4.5:1 on white, so it clears AA on white and nothing else — 3.86:1 on `--wash-2`, 3.84:1 on the dark header. The correction runs in **opposite directions** on the two grounds. A 6px dot has no text on it and was wearing the compromise for no reason: the brand orange appeared in 3 places on the page and the darkened one in 14. | QA §1, §4r |
| C2 | **Cool neutrals, never warm.** | Warm off-white reads as AI-generated. | — |
| C3 | **State layers are composited, not picked.** Hover and selected are one translucent layer at two alphas; `#E7EBF0` is *hover*, `#DEE4EB` is *selected*. | No screenshot can tell you this, and using hover for selected makes every selected row a step too light. | QA §4f |
| C4 | **A photographic ground's veil is computed** — `1 − target ÷ that image's mean luminance` — so images of different brightness land a screen on the same value. | | design-system §15 |
| C5 | **Translucent chrome over an image must be safe by construction**: compute against white, the lightest thing it can sit on. Blur makes it read as frosted; transparency is not what does that. | axe cannot evaluate a scrolling backdrop. | QA §4k4 |
| C6 | **axe 0 violations.** Audit the *resting* frame — park any loop first, then sample ~20 times across it. | | QA §1 |
| C11 | **Never put `opacity` on `--ink-mute`.** It is defined as the lightest text the page allows on every ground it uses; any opacity on top of it is under AA by construction. Make a label quieter with size or weight. | `.vs__vs` shipped at `opacity:.5` and axe caught it. | QA §1 |
| C7 | **A component that crosses grounds carries both versions as a token swap**, never as a second copy. The header defines `--nav-ground` / `--nav-ink` / `--nav-ink-hi` / `--nav-accent` / `--nav-chip`; no rule inside it names a colour. | Two copies drift the moment one of them is edited. | QA §4o |
| C8 | **A colour used at an alpha is declared as channels** — `--ink-rgb`, `--paper-rgb`, `--accent-rgb`, `--accent-solid-rgb`, `--ok-rgb`, `--wait-rgb` — and consumed as `rgb(var(--ink-rgb) / .05)`. A hex cannot carry an alpha, which is the whole reason the literals bred. | `rgba(12,15,18,…)` was re-typed **41 times in 22 different alphas**, and not one of them would have followed `--ink` if `--ink` had moved. | QA §4r |
| C10 | **An ink tuned against one ground is a colour plus an assumption.** Any ink that sits on a tint (`--accent-solid`, `--accent-wash`, `--ok-ink`, `--wait-ink`) needs a sibling for the other mode, and the correction usually REVERSES direction. | Three separate tokens have now needed this, each found only after dark mode existed: 3.86:1, 3.96:1, 2.3:1. | QA §1, §4s |
| C13 | **Bright is for fields, deep is for marks.** Five of the ten brand colours are bright: measured on `--wash-2` the raw hues run 1.11:1 (yellow) to 1.74 (orange), so a 3px marker in them is invisible and a large field in them shouts. Every scene carries a `--hue-*` for grounds and a `--mark-*` carried toward the ink until it clears 3:1. | Tong: *"太亮的颜色不适合大面积使用"*. A whole viewport of the accent was the hero, and it is the brand off-white now — the largest field on the page is the calmest colour in the palette. | QA §1 |
| C14 | **A ground marker outlives the ground.** `data-ground="dark"` told the header to invert; the closing band and footer had since gone white, so the bar was flipping to its dark version over a light page. Check the ground still IS what the marker says before trusting it. | | QA §4o |
| C12 | **A tint INSIDE a mock is `--tile`, never `--wash`.** `--wash` is the grey the *page* sits on: in dark it goes **darker** than `--paper`, so every surface the product raises above its card comes out as a hole punched in it. | The grant dialog shipped its target pill, icon slot and scope chip that way. It is invisible in light, which is why it shipped. | QA §4s |
| C9 | **The page has two modes and they are ONE token swap** — eleven grounds and inks redefined under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, plus a `[data-theme]` pin so it can be forced. No second stylesheet, no `dark:` variant per rule. What carries over is the RELATIONSHIPS, not the values: a card is one step off the page ground, the header one step off the card, the band stays the darkest surface. Product mocks do **not** invert — they draw the app, and a real screenshot does not flip with the page around it (P1). | A second copy of a design drifts from the first the week after it is written. | QA §1, §4s |

## Motion

| # | Rule | Why | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | **Transform, opacity and clip-path only** for anything scroll-linked, looping, or per-frame. Tokenised curves; never `ease`/`linear`. **One written exception:** the header's rest↔float change transitions `top`/`left`/`right`/`height`/`border-radius`. | The rule exists because layout properties cost a reflow *every frame*. This is a one-shot 420ms state change on a single fixed element that fires twice per visit, not a timeline — and the alternative (a transform-driven inset) cannot hold `--r-section` because scaling a box scales its corner. Exceptions are allowed; unexamined ones are not. | QA §4o |
| N2 | **The resting state is the finished state.** `.is-live` is added by JS only, so reduced motion, no-JS and pre-observer all show complete content. | Never build a loop that leaves the page empty if it fails. | QA §5 |
| N3 | **Looping text must not fade.** Reveal text-bearing elements with `clip-path` — painted or not painted, never half-legible. | Once per load is an artifact; every twelve seconds forever is a defect. | QA §1 |
| N4 | **One rAF and one cue list per timeline.** Never a stack of `setTimeout`s. Gate on an observer and on `visibilitychange`. | | motion.md |
| N5 | **Lead with the hero**: the most important element gets the largest displacement and the only sprung curve. **One spatial origin** — mixed directions read as chaos. **Stagger so each beat settles before the next starts: for separate OBJECTS the delay is 25-35% of the duration.** Words in one sentence and lines in one headline are exempt — they overlap ~90% on purpose, because a sentence rising is one gesture, not a queue. | LottieFiles motion-design. Three tiles at 70ms against a 560ms fade arrived as one blur; the rule was there and unmeasured. | QA §7 |
| N6 | **Write the irregularity down.** Four tasks finishing 1-3-2-4 read as independent runs; 1-2-3-4 reads as a progress bar. | | — |
| N7 | **Anything present-but-not-showing must be `inert`**, not merely `aria-hidden` — a product mock is full of real buttons. | | QA §4k2 |
| N8 | **Auto-advancing anything pauses** off screen, in a background tab, on keyboard focus, and under reduced motion; a click parks it for good. It does **not** pause on hover. | The progress is what says the panel will change; freezing it under the pointer makes the section feel stuck. A keyboard user has no other way to hold it. | QA §4m |
| N10 | **A loop that returns to its start takes a symmetric curve** (`--e-inout`), and it is TWO keyframes with `alternate` — never three stops with an ease-out. A timing function applies between each pair of keyframes, so a multi-stop loop restarts its easing at every stop and reads as a series of darts. | The scroll hint ran 0/45/70/100 on `--e-elegant`: three ease-outs per cycle, plus a 30% dead hold and an opacity pulse on a fourth rhythm. | QA §7 |
| N11 | **A layout property is never a side effect of an animation.** Centring, offsets and sizing are static declarations; keyframes only ever carry the movement. | The scroll hint's `translateX(-50%)` lived inside its keyframes. Replacing the keyframes silently un-centred the pill, and the change under test — the motion — was correct, so nothing caught it. | QA §7 |
| N12 | **Arm a reveal transition one painted frame after the state class.** Add `.is-live`, run the first cue pass, then add the class that carries the `transition`. | The resting state is the finished state (N2), so switching both on together plays the loop's *opening* state as an animation — nine open rows sliding shut in front of the reader before anything has started. | motion.md |
| N22 | **A message that has not arrived takes no room.** In a playing conversation, collapse the rows that have not been said yet — do not merely clip them. | Clipped at full height they held their space at the bottom of a bottom-anchored list, so the conversation played in the top half against 130px of reserved nothing and the messages that HAD arrived were the ones pushed off. Collapsed, `flex-end` does the whole job: each message appears on the composer's edge and lifts the ones before it. | QA §4m3 |
| N23 | **A conversation reserves no strip for something that may not be there.** The typing indicator is a message *inside* the list, collapsed when idle — never a fixed row outside it. | A fixed 17px "is typing" row outside the list reserved 19px whether or not anyone was typing, on every tab, always. (This rule also used to say the conversation ends on the composer's edge with nothing between them; that part was wrong and is now M8.) | QA §4m3 |
| N24 | **A hold starts when the thing is on screen, not when the page loads.** | The 1.5s before the channel lifted was spent long before anyone had scrolled that far, so it was always already lifted by the time it was looked at — the bug, restored, by the fix for it. | QA §4m3 |
| N13 | **A collapsible row's air is leading, never padding.** A `0fr` grid track is floored at the padding box of the track *and* of the item inside it. | 3px above and below kept a "closed" row 6px tall with its label still painted, overlapping its neighbour's for the whole transition. | QA §4k |
| N14 | **A marquee inside a padded container starts one item in.** Travelling from 0 leaves the container's inset strip with content behind it all cycle and nothing behind it at the reset. Prove the wrap by diffing the two frames, not by watching for it. | A sliver of ground popping in a corner is the only thing in the frame moving discontinuously, which is exactly what the eye catches. | QA §4l2 |
| N15 | **Whatever must be visible outside the text column belongs on the element that clips.** A live-row highlight set as a negative inline margin on the row *inside* the shutter is wider than the box whose `overflow:hidden` makes the shutter work, so the clip takes its corners off. | It shipped as a full-bleed rectangle for two rounds and `getComputedStyle` reported the radius the whole time. | QA §4k |
| N16 | **A loop's elapsed time survives a pause.** Resuming from zero rewinds every mechanic at once. | The observer fires at 25% visible, so a reset on re-entry is watched, not hidden. | — |
| N18 | **A vignette belongs to the thing being vignetted, not to the frame around it.** A `mask-image` on a band masks the band's own fill, so that band can never carry a ground. | It cost the connector card its tint, and the card its boundary against its own sentence. | QA §4f |
| N19 | **A fade over discrete objects has to reach zero before anything clips them.** One straight alpha ramp leaves the outer objects part-painted when `overflow:hidden` cuts them, and a half-painted object chopped in half is what reads as abrupt. | Widening the gradient makes it worse, not better: the objects then arrive at the edge at full strength. | QA §4f |
| N17 | **A layout that has to fit is a flex column, not a comment full of arithmetic.** Let the one element that can absorb the slack flex; state the rest. | Two rounds of hand-solving a 276px band both ended with the chart's baseline behind the receipt. Any number derived in prose stops being true when a font loads or a label wraps. | QA §4f |
| N21 | **Size the box for the FINISHED state and let what leaves live outside it.** An element that ends smaller than it starts is laid out at its end size, with the departing parts drawn beyond the box on `overflow:visible`. | The wordmark opens as OKOU and gathers to the mark. Animating the element's width instead would reflow the header every frame for the whole gather, and the nav's centred links would drift; measured at 390/768/1440, the box stays 26px and the links do not move. | QA §4ab |
| N20 | **A phase class is cleared by whatever clears the class that set it.** A multi-phase entrance adds `.is-live` for the opening state and a second class to release it; the release class has to come off in the same place `.is-live` does. | Left behind on the hand-back, the next tab opened already landed and never played its opening beat — and every tab after it inherited the same. | QA §4ab |
| N9 | **A looping reel clones, and only the middle set is real.** Clones are `aria-hidden`, roleless and out of the tab order; selection is marked on the centred slot, never by matching a data attribute. | Matching lights every copy and shows a second highlighted item at the mask edge — the seam the clones exist to hide. | QA §4m |

## Product mocks

## Process

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Edit the source, never `site/styles.css`.** Run `tools/build-css.py`, which stamps `?r=` on CSS/JS and `?v=<sha1>` on **every local asset**. | A hand-kept hash shipped stale CSS across four deploys; unversioned images then made two correct screenshot fixes invisible for two rounds. |
| R7 | **Verify a fix against the SHIPPED bytes, not your build.** `curl` the live URL, check its hash, crop the image and look at it. | A local check cannot detect a delivery bug — the file was right on disk and right on the server, and still wrong on screen. |
| R8 | **Cache-bust the page URL when verifying in a browser**, not just the asset hash. `?r=` changes on the stylesheet, but a cached HTML never asks for the new one — three rebuilds in a row measured identical wrong numbers against correct CSS. | | QA §4t |
| R10 | **Measure a mock's colours in BOTH themes before believing it.** Mock surfaces are `aria-hidden`, so axe never checks them. | Three separate defects hid behind a correct-looking light screenshot: mock text at 1.92:1 in dark, a terminal at 1.16:1 against its band, and a mask still 60% opaque where the card cut it. | QA §4y |
| R2 | **Never slice a file between two anchors without asserting the distance.** Prefer `replace(old, new, 1)` with the full block and an assert. | A two-anchor slice once deleted 1084 lines, and the symptom surfaced far from the cause. |
| R9 | **Never regex across nested tags of the same name**, and run `tools/check-html.py` after any scripted markup edit. | `<div class="vsui">.*?</div>` stopped at the first nested `</div>` and left an orphan that closed `.versus` early. | R3 | **`git diff --stat` before building**, and read the built file for the rule you just wrote — the pruner has silently eaten one before. | |
